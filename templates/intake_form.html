Operational Safeguards

  • Non-Authoritative Triage: The AI extracts entities for human triage; it cannot authorize payouts, create finance entries, or resolve claims.
  • Zero Currency Assumption: Currency is extracted only when explicitly stated in the message; never inferred or defaulted to INR.
  • Automatic Review Routing: Messages with missing references, disputes, or adversarial text are queued for human review.
