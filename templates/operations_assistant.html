Refund Operations Assistant

Operational guidance directory and automated workflow management. To guarantee financial data integrity and eliminate conversational hallucinations, the platform provides specialized, deterministic workflows with human-in-the-loop oversight rather than an unconstrained chatbot.

Architecture: Purpose-Built Workflows AI Backend: Groq LLM (Strict Schema) Safety: Deterministic Financial Verification

Operational Policy & System Boundary: The platform does not provide an open-ended conversational bot or automated payment disbursement. All AI operations are strictly restricted to structured message intake, validation, and human review triage. Below is the verified directory of currently active tools and planned capabilities.

AI Refund Request Intake

Live

Processes raw unstructured refund communications from customer emails, WhatsApp chats, and manual desk notes. Extracts booking reference, customer name, flight route, claim amount, currency, and complaint category using Groq LLM with strict Pydantic validation.

Human Oversight Review Queue

Live

Centralized triage queue capturing flagged customer requests requiring operator intervention. Isolates claims with missing identifiers, amount disputes, lack of progress, or low extraction confidence before any downstream processing.

Cross-Department Reconciliation

Live

Deterministic cross-department engine matching Support Tracker tickets against Finance Tracker bank disbursements. Identifies status mismatches, unbilled refunds, untracked payments, duplicate reference claims, and ambiguous candidates.

Open Reconciliation Engine →

Partner Dispute Escalations

Live

Comprehensive audit ledger of B2B travel partner escalations. Tracks aging duration, communication channels, complaint categories, and partner resolution statuses with clean separation of open vs. resolved disputes.
